BLOCK INC. · FEATURED GAME

GeoSprint

GeoSprint is an original arcade platform game created by Block Inc. It brings fast-paced movement, timing-based challenges and a bold geometric art direction together in a focused, energetic experience.

Although it belongs to the broad arcade-platform genre, GeoSprint has its own presentation, assets, identity and creative direction. Players are invited to sharpen their reflexes, learn each challenge and push for a better run.
